About Ubuntu Urban Wear

Ubuntu Urban Wear is a proposed South African retail organisation specialising in affordable contemporary and streetwear clothing. The brand aims to provide young consumers with stylish products and a convenient digital shopping experience.

Mission

To provide affordable, stylish and accessible fashion while delivering a convenient shopping experience.

Vision

To become a recognised South African online fashion brand known for quality, affordability and customer service.

Target Audience

South African students, young professionals and digitally active consumers aged approximately 18–35 who value affordability, convenience and modern style.
